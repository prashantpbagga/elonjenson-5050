Eligibility

Who can apply for OCI?

OCI is for foreign nationals with Indian roots. You can apply if any of the following are true:

  • You were a citizen of India on or after 26 January 1950.
  • You were eligible to become a citizen of India on 26 January 1950.
  • You belonged to a territory that became part of India after 15 August 1947.
  • You are a child, grandchild, or great-grandchild of any of the above.
  • You are a minor child of an Indian citizen, or of an existing OCI cardholder.
  • You are the spouse of an Indian citizen or OCI cardholder (married ≥ 2 years).

Not eligible: Citizens of Pakistan and Bangladesh, or anyone whose parents or grandparents were such citizens.

Documents you'll need

  1. Current foreign passport (bio page)
  2. Proof of Indian origin (old Indian passport, birth certificate, etc.)
  3. Recent passport-style photograph (200 × 200 px, ≤ 200 KB)
  4. Signature scan (white background, ≤ 200 KB)
  5. Proof of current address
  6. If applying as spouse: marriage certificate and spouse's Indian/OCI documents

How to apply

Four steps from start to OCI card

    Start your application

    Create an account, fill Part A (personal details) and Part B (additional details). Auto-save kicks in every 30 seconds.

FAQ

Common questions

Is OCI the same as dual citizenship?

No. India does not allow dual citizenship. OCI is a lifelong visa with most rights of a citizen, except voting, holding constitutional office, and purchasing agricultural land.

Do I need to re-issue OCI when I get a new passport?

Yes, re-issuance is mandatory each time a new passport is issued up to age 20, and once after age 50. Between 21 and 50, re-issuance is recommended but not mandatory.

How long is the OCI card valid?

It is a lifelong multi-entry visa, subject to passport re-issuance rules above.

Can OCI holders work in India?

Yes, OCI holders may work in India in any profession except as judges, members of legislative bodies, or in roles requiring Indian citizenship.

What is the fee for an OCI application?

USD 275 for new applications (or equivalent in local currency). Re-issuance is USD 100. PIO to OCI conversion is free.

What if my application is rejected?

You will receive a written reason and may reapply after addressing the issue. Appeals can be made to the Ministry of Home Affairs within 30 days.
